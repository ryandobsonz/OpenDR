DARK REIGN: THE FUTURE OF WAR
=============================

The original Dark Reign campaigns, Freedom Guard and Imperium, on the OpenRA
engine at modern resolutions: fullscreen, borderless or windowed, ultrawide
and 4K included.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------

Your own copy of Dark Reign: the 1.8.2 community patch or the GOG release.
No game data comes with this; it is copied from your copy when you install.
Nothing else needs installing.


GETTING STARTED
---------------

1. Run DarkReign.exe.

2. Choose INSTALL FROM GAME and pick your Dark Reign folder: the one that
   holds the game's "dark" folder. A GOG install is found for you. This
   copies the graphics, sounds and music and converts the campaign missions,
   in about a minute.

3. Choose PLAY. The campaigns are under Missions.


SETTINGS
--------

SETTINGS in the launcher chooses:

  Display mode      Fullscreen, Borderless (recommended) or Windowed
  Monitor           Which screen the game opens on
  Resolution        The screen's resolutions for fullscreen, or a window size
  Interface size    How large the sidebar and menus are; raise it on 4K
  Battlefield zoom  How much of the battlefield is in view. Close is about
                    the original game's 640 x 480 view
  VSync             Matches the screen's refresh rate

The game's own Settings > Display menu changes the same values.


WHERE THINGS ARE
----------------

Settings, saved games, logs and the installed game data are kept in
%APPDATA%\OpenRA. If the game closes unexpectedly, the launcher offers to
open the logs.

CAMPAIGN.md describes how the missions are converted and what differs from
the original game.
